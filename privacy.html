Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 19, 2026

Dear Next Chapter Spreadsheets ("the app") is a private tool operated by Dear Next Chapter. It is used only by the owner of the Dear Next Chapter Google account to create spreadsheet templates.

What the app accesses

  • Google Sheets — to create and edit spreadsheets.
  • Google Drive (files created by the app only) — to save the spreadsheets it creates. The app cannot see or open any other files in Google Drive.

How data is used

Data is used only to build spreadsheets in the owner's own Google Drive. Sign-in tokens are stored locally on the owner's computer.

Sharing

The app does not sell, share, or transfer any Google user data to third parties, and does not use it for advertising.

Google API Services User Data Policy

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
